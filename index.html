FaceTime Privacy Policy

Please read this policy completely before continuing.

What FaceTime with Arven does

FaceTime with Arven uses your device camera so Arven can receive visual information during the FaceTime session. Your camera preview is shown on your device at a normal live frame rate, while visual frames sent for AI processing are limited to approximately one frame per second.

Face recognition and your name

When you first use FaceTime, Arven can detect a face on your device and create a mathematical face template, also called a face embedding. The original camera photograph is not stored as your registered face profile. The template is associated with a name that you provide and synchronized with your Arven account.

Biometric template protection

Face templates are sensitive information. Arven stores the registered template in encrypted form using AES-256-GCM on the backend before it is written to Firebase Realtime Database. The encryption key is kept on the backend server and is not stored in the database or frontend.

What is not stored

FaceTime does not intentionally store the raw camera frames used for FaceTime visual processing. Camera frames are transient input for the live session.

Multiple people on one account

An Arven account can contain multiple registered face profiles. If a face does not sufficiently match a registered profile, Arven asks for a name. A name already associated with another registered face cannot be reused for a new face profile.

Camera controls

You can exit FaceTime at any time. The back-camera control becomes available only after a face has been successfully registered. Camera permission is controlled by your device and browser.

Deleting a registered face

You can review registered FaceTime faces in FaceTime Face Settings and delete a face profile.

Privacy acceptance and security

Accepting this policy is recorded against your authenticated Arven account. The backend independently checks this consent before allowing FaceTime biometric operations. Editing or replacing the frontend cannot make an account eligible for FaceTime without the server-side consent record.

Where can I get help if anything goes wrong

If FaceTime is not working correctly or you need support, visit Fluxionary Support.
